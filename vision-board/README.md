# Vision board: May 2029

`Vision_Board_May_2029.png` (2400×1520, for a screen or wallpaper) and `Vision_Board_May_2029.pdf` (A3 landscape, for printing).

The message: protect the capital, follow the plan, take small daily wins, and walk out of the rat race by the first week of May 2029.

- **The art:** the rat-race wheel with an opening, a walker carrying the CAPITAL shield up ~640 small steps (a few small red ones, never a cliff) to the May 2029 flag at sunrise. The red dashed line is the gamble: a spike, then a crash to −46%.
- **Card 1:** recovery math. A loss of 46% needs +85% just to get back.
- **Card 2:** the daily rules from the Guruji manual's risk card (1% risk = ₹2,700 on ₹2,70,000, daily stop, max 3 trades, flat by 14:45 on expiry Tuesday).
- **Card 3:** small daily wins, singles not sixes.
- **Card 4:** a 31-month discipline tracker (Oct 2026 to Apr 2029, then May 2029) to tick by hand.

Rebuild: `python3 build_board.py` (uses the Chromium at /opt/pw-browsers). Capital, risk and session count are constants at the top of the script.
